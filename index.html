WHAT IS EGGSPLODE?

Eggsplode! is a fan-made ROBLOX revival project inspired by the classic Roblox experience from 2014 to 2017. Our goal is to recreate the feeling of old Roblox with the simple website, classic games, and community-driven atmosphere. Eggsplode! is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation. It is a separate project built by fans who enjoy the older Roblox era and want to bring back that nostalgic experience.
